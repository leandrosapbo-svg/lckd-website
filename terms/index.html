1 Studio Operations

Locked Fitness Center, operating under the brands LCKD and LCKD LAB (collectively referred to as “LCKD”), reserves the right to make reasonable operational adjustments when necessary.

These may include changes to class schedules, coaches, training programs, facilities, membership pricing, partnerships, and special initiatives.

Any changes will be made with the goal of maintaining or improving the overall member experience. Members will be notified in advance of material changes affecting their existing memberships wherever reasonably possible.

2 Photography & Video Recording

LCKD may capture photographs and videos during regular classes, events, and studio activities for social media, advertising, and promotional purposes.

Members who do not wish to appear in promotional content may inform the reception team or management.

LCKD will respect these preferences and avoid featuring members who have opted out.

Photography and video recording are strictly prohibited during Ladies Only classes, including recording by members, coaches, and staff.

Members must obtain permission before photographing or recording other members.

LCKD will obtain any consent required by applicable law before using identifiable member images for promotional purposes.

3 Injuries, Medical Conditions & Assistance

Member safety is a priority at LCKD.

Members must inform their coach of any relevant injury, medical condition, pregnancy, physical limitation, or other concern that may affect safe participation.

Members are responsible for seeking medical clearance when appropriate.

In the event of an injury or medical emergency, staff may provide basic assistance and contact emergency medical services when necessary.

LCKD coaches are fitness professionals and are not acting as medical practitioners.

Members must follow coaching instructions, equipment safety guidelines, and studio rules.

Members should stop exercising immediately if they experience pain, dizziness, unusual discomfort, or other concerning symptoms.

4 Class Bookings, Cancellations & No-Shows

Classes must be booked through the official LCKD booking system or another approved method.

Members must cancel their bookings at least 1 hour before the scheduled class.

Late cancellations and no-shows may result in the deduction of a class credit or booking restrictions.

Repeated late cancellations or no-shows may result in temporary booking restrictions.

Class availability is subject to studio capacity and scheduling.

Memberships and class packages are personal and non-transferable unless expressly approved by management.

LCKD reserves the right to cancel or reschedule classes when reasonably necessary. Where LCKD cancels a booked class, any deducted class credit will be restored or an appropriate alternative provided.

5 Membership Duration, Cancellation & Freezing

1-Month Membership

Freezing is not permitted except for a documented medical injury or illness, subject to management approval.

3-Month Membership

May be frozen for a maximum of 10 calendar days during the membership term.

6-Month Membership

May be frozen for a maximum of 20 calendar days during the membership term.

12-Month Membership

May be frozen for a maximum of 20 calendar days during the membership term.

General Conditions

Freeze requests must be submitted and approved in advance.

Medical freeze requests require a valid medical certificate.

Freezes cannot be backdated or transferred to another membership.

Approved freezes extend the membership expiry date by the approved number of days, unless otherwise stated in the applicable membership agreement.

Early cancellation of a fixed-term membership may result in an early termination charge equal to the outstanding contractual balance, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Any statutory cancellation or refund rights remain unaffected.

6 Payments, Refunds & Membership Fees

All membership fees must be paid in full or according to an agreed payment plan.

Membership prices are expressed in Qatari Riyals (QAR).

Memberships and class packages are non-refundable except where required by applicable law or expressly approved by LCKD management.

LCKD reserves the right to change prices for future purchases and renewals.

Existing fixed-term membership prices will remain as agreed for the contracted period unless the agreement expressly provides otherwise.

Failure to complete an agreed payment may result in suspension of booking privileges until the outstanding amount is settled.

Any recurring payment arrangement must be disclosed and agreed to at the time of purchase.

7 Member Conduct & Studio Etiquette

LCKD is committed to providing a respectful, motivating, inclusive, and safe training environment.

All members must:

  • Treat coaches, staff, and fellow members with respect.
  • Wear appropriate training attire and suitable footwear for the activity.
  • Use equipment correctly and follow coaching instructions.
  • Arrive on time and respect class schedules.
  • Maintain personal hygiene and leave equipment clean after use.
  • Respect studio property, changing rooms, and shared facilities.
  • Refrain from harassment, threatening conduct, discrimination, or inappropriate behaviour.
  • Avoid using equipment or participating in classes while under the influence of alcohol or impairing substances.

LCKD reserves the right to suspend or terminate access for serious or repeated violations, subject to applicable law.

Members should review the Privacy Policy for information about data processing, retention, and applicable rights.
