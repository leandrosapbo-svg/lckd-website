1About these terms

LCKD Admin is the private app, at lckd.qa/admin, that the team at Locked Fitness Center (“LCKD”) uses to run its classes. It lets coaches see the day's bookings and students, look up a student's history, see celebrations such as birthdays and milestone classes, message students about their bookings, and keep notes on students.

These terms apply to everyone who signs in. By signing in you agree to them. If you do not agree, do not use the app. How the app handles personal information is explained in the Privacy Policy.

2Who may use the app

  • The app is for internal use only: LCKD coaches, staff and management who have been given access.
  • Access is given by the owner, who adds your Google account to the coach list. You can only sign in with that account. Without being on the list, you cannot use the app.
  • Your access is personal. Do not let anyone else use your sign-in or your signed-in device.
  • Members of LCKD and the public are not users of this app and cannot create an account.

3What the app is for

Use the app only to coach, look after students and run LCKD. Do not use it for any other purpose, for your own business, or to look up people out of curiosity.

4Your responsibilities

  • Keep your Google account secure. Use a screen lock on your phone, and sign out on any shared or borrowed device.
  • Tell the owner straight away if you lose a device, think someone else has used your sign-in, or notice something wrong in the app.
  • Do not try to get around the app's protections, open data you are not meant to see, or disturb how the app works.
  • Everything you do in the app, including the notes you write, is recorded under your name.

5Student information and notes

  • Student information (names, phone numbers, bookings, attendance, and notes) is confidential. Use it only to do your job at LCKD.
  • Do not copy, screenshot, export or share it outside LCKD, except as needed to coach or look after a student.
  • Notes must be factual, respectful and relevant to training. Write only what you need to. Do not record more sensitive detail than necessary, such as medical information beyond what a student has told us and what helps us coach them safely.
  • Notes are visible to all LCKD coaches and management and show your name and the date. They belong to LCKD's records for the student and may stay after you stop working with LCKD. You can hide a note you wrote, but hidden notes are still kept in LCKD's records.

6Messaging students

The WhatsApp button only prepares a chat with a message about the student's booking. You send it from your own WhatsApp. Use it for LCKD matters such as bookings, classes and the studio, not for personal or unrelated messages. Respect a student who asks you to stop.

7Access can be removed

The owner can remove your access at any time, with or without notice, for example when you stop working with LCKD or if these terms are not followed. Once removed you can no longer sign in, and any sign-in you already have stops working within moments.

8Availability and accuracy

The app is provided as it is. Its booking information is copied from LCKD's booking system (G8TS) every few minutes, so it can be a little behind or incomplete. For anything important, such as a payment or a disputed booking, check the booking system itself. The app may sometimes be unavailable, for example during an update or if a service it relies on is down.
