1What LCKD Admin does

LCKD Admin helps the LCKD team run its classes day to day. Coaches use it on their phones and computers to:

  • See the day's classes and how many people are booked, how full each zone is (Strength, Cardio, Hybrid) and how people booked (free trial, walk-in, membership, class pack or the LCKD booking app).
  • See who is in each class, with their spot, so the coach knows who is coming.
  • Look at one student: their plan, how many classes they attended, their first and last visit, their usual class time and favourite zone, and their recent classes.
  • Search students by name or phone number.
  • Spot celebrations: a student's birthday and milestone classes (for example the 10th or 25th class).
  • Message a student on WhatsApp about their booking. The app only prepares the chat and the message text. The coach sends it from their own WhatsApp.
  • Keep notes on students, in categories, so every coach can see what the others noted. Each note shows the coach's name and the date.
  • Manage who can use the app. The owner adds and removes coaches.

2Information the app uses

About coaches and staff

  • The name and email address of your Google account, received when you sign in with Google.
  • When you last signed in, and the notes you write (shown with your name).

About students

This comes from LCKD's own booking system (G8TS), which students use to book classes:

  • Name, phone number and gender (if the student has given it).
  • Class bookings and attendance, including no-shows and cancellations, and how each class was booked.
  • Their membership or class pack, and how many classes they have attended.
  • Whether today is their birthday. The app does not store anyone's date of birth. It only marks a student on the day their birthday falls.
  • Notes that coaches write about them (for example goals, progress or an injury the student has told us about).

What the app does not use

The app does not collect payment card details, health questionnaires, photos, location or advertising data. It does not set advertising or tracking cookies. The only cookies are the one that keeps a coach signed in and a short-lived one used while signing in with Google.

3Signing in with Google

Coaches sign in with their own Google account, so that LCKD knows who is using the app and whose name goes on each note.

  • We receive only your name and email address (and Google's confirmation that the email is verified). We ask Google for nothing else.
  • We do not read or access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.
  • Your name and email are used only to let you in and to label your notes. They are not used for advertising and are not sold or shared.
  • Only people the owner has added to the coach list can get in. A Google account that is not on the list is refused.

LCKD's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4How we use the information

Only to run LCKD: to prepare and coach classes, to look after students (for example by knowing about an injury or a goal), to answer students' questions about their bookings, to celebrate milestones, and to control who can use the app. We do not sell the information, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions about people with it.

5Where it is kept and who can see it

A sync tool run by LCKD copies booking information from the LCKD booking system into LCKD's private storage on Cloudflare, which also hosts lckd.qa. The app shows it only to coaches who are signed in. Each coach has their own sign-in. Students are identified in the app by a private code, and we do not keep their booking-system PINs.

Students and the public cannot open the app. Notes are visible to all LCKD coaches and management, and to no one else.

6Who we share it with

We do not sell or give the information to anyone. The services that help us run the app can process it on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and stores its data.
  • Google confirms who a coach is when they sign in.
  • G8TS is LCKD's booking system, where the booking information comes from.
  • WhatsApp carries a message only when a coach chooses to send one to a student.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

7How long we keep it

  • Booking information copied from the booking system is removed automatically about 13 months after it was last updated.
  • Notes are kept as part of LCKD's records for the student, and are not deleted automatically. A coach can hide a note they wrote, and the owner can hide any note. A hidden note disappears from the app but is kept in our records. A coach can also edit a note they wrote, and the earlier version is kept in our records too.
  • Coach details are kept while the coach has access. When a coach is removed they can no longer sign in, and their name and email stay in our records so that the notes they wrote still show who wrote them.

8Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you in the app, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We will act on your request unless we need to keep the information for a legal or record-keeping reason, and we will tell you if that is the case. We handle personal data in line with the data protection laws of Qatar. To make a request, write to contact@lckd.qa.

9Security

The app is private and protected by sign-in. Each coach uses their own Google account, the owner can remove a coach at any time (they are signed out within moments), and the connection to the app is encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep access limited to the people who need it.

10Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@lckd.qa, or in writing to Locked Fitness Center, Porto Arabia La Crosset 22, The Pearl, Qatar.
